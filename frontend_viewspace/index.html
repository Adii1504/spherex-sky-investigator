Astronomical Analysis

Barnard's Star is a nearby red dwarf star with the largest known proper motion of any star. SPHEREx observations spanning 2025 to 2026 clearly capture its real celestial motion (+9.0'' displacement in Declination), creating an unambiguous dipole signature in difference imaging matching Gaia DR3 ground truth within sub-pixel accuracy.
