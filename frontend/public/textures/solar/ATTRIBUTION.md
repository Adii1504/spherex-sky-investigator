# Solar System surface maps

Textures by Solar System Scope / INOVE, based on NASA imagery and elevation data.
Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original 2k JPEGs are stored here without image modifications. Rendering
maps them onto spheres with lighting and rotation. Venus uses the atmosphere map.
The source notes that colors are enhanced and unmapped gaps can contain inferred
terrain. These are educational surface reconstructions, not live imagery.
